## General Resources

- **Learn to Code for Beginners:** 
    - [freecodecamp.org](https://www.freecodecamp.org/)
    - [scratch.mit.edu](https://scratch.mit.edu/)
    - [learnpython.org](https://www.learnpython.org/)

- [w3schools](https://www.w3schools.com/) - Like a dictionary for coding- complete courses, quizzes, or tutorials, or use it to look for examples, explanations, and syntax for your code.

- [Harvard CS50 Introduction to Computer Science](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/) - Slightly more advanced; this is an introduction to computer science course offered by Harvard, but the full 11 weeks of material is available for free here. 

- [The Odin Project](https://www.theodinproject.com/) - Similar to freecodecamp, but with only one pathway. Developed and maintained for free by developers who have picked the order of topics based on how they wish they had learned to code.

- [leetcode](https://leetcode.com/) - A bank of coding puzzles to try. Sorted by difficult from beginner to advanced, but some coding knowledge is needed first. Your solutions are tested and there is a forum to ask for help if you need it.
