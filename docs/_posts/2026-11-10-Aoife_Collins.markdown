---

We then did a mario kart Tournament and I came first in every race. I got into the final and won. we then just played a few more races before lunch.

## Wednesday


---


![Red Hat Tys](https://media.licdn.com/dms/image/D4E12AQGU2MRA1t_flw/article-cover_image-shrink_720_1280/0/1669889882460?e=2147483647&v=beta&t=2iisPY76v14iDs2r6ruxcI0rKQ5a51bWC5Ted8bh6Fc "Red Hat TYs")



On Wednesday we did a stand up first thing in the morning. After the stand up, we learned about bash and how to use it. we tried all the different commands and also did a quiz.

we drew out an idea and started building it. we finished just before 4 o clock.
