## TY Work Experience At RedHat!

This was a very enjoyable week where I learned lots more about technology, like Python and Agile.


## Monday


---


![Red Hat Office](https://ctsgroup.ie/images/made/images/uploads/clients/IMG_0606_960_550_s_c1.JPG "Red Hat Waterford")


On Monday, we came in and got a tour of the office. Then we had a very important health and safety meeting. After that we did some ice breakers. They were:

* 2 truths and a lie. 
* What would you do if the internet broke for the day?
* A Mario Kart tournament.

I enjoyed the Mario Kart Tournament but I wasn't very good. After lunch we learned about Agile and scrum. We did a project involving Mr. Potato Head. I was the scrum master who had to write up if certain stages needed to be done, in progress or completed.


---


## Tuesday


---


![Red Hat](https://media.licdn.com/dms/image/sync/v2/D4E27AQG0k7J11PhVrA/articleshare-shrink_800/articleshare-shrink_800/0/1715854575117?e=2147483647&v=beta&t=p90eVR4DoE3f_dLfR9lHtLAVEG56CL9iItgiYbWf0yU "Red Hat Waterford")


On Tuesday, we came in and had a standup. This was to recap what we did on Monday and what we will do on Wednesday. Then we were told about GitHub. After we created a GitHub account, we started work on our blogs. Then we learned about Python. We learned about:

* Values and variables
* If conditionals and statements
* For loops 

After lunch, we did a Monopoly exercise and I came 4th. The Monopoly exercise was that you started on 50 euro and then you rolled a dice a certain amount of times and you either lost or gained money based on the roll. Then we cracked a million passwords(not actually). This showed us more aspects of Python.

---


## Wednesday


---



On Wednesday, we came in and had another standup. Then we did some more work on our blogs. After that, we learned about Bash and CLI and we played a game called overthewire. Next, we had a talk about cloud computing. This talk involved:

* Jobs in technology/coding.
* Past, present and future of technology
* AI datacentres

Then the interns told us about there life and how they got into RedHat. After that, we did a group activity where we made a rollercoaster out of straws. This was to teach us about teamwork. 


---


## Thursday


---


![Terminal](https://helpdeskgeek.com/wp-content/pictures/2023/01/preview-media-0-Title-Image.jpg "Terminal")


On Thursday, we came in and had a Town Hall. This is where people from RedHat and IBM come into one room and talk about any new changes or any news. Then there was breakfast. Next, we had a standup. After that, we learned about Zooniverse, which is a website where people can help researchers by looking at photos and answering questions on it. Then we had a talk on AI and how to use it correctly. We learned about Notebook and all of its features.

After lunch we did a sustainability project. Our project was on Precision Agriculture and IoT for Sustainable Farming. When everyone was finished presenting, we did a few minutes on our blogs.
